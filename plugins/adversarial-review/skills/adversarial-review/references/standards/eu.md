# European Union

EU law applies to anyone offering goods or services to people in the EU. Member states add their own
rules on top. How to read and refresh this table is in [../cache.md](../cache.md).

| ID | Standard or obligation | Kind | Use when | Cached note | Checked |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `EU-GDPR` | [General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj) | Regulation | Any personal data of people in the EU | In force 24 May 2016, applies from 25 May 2018 | 1 Nov 2026 |
| `EU-EPRIV` | [ePrivacy Directive 2002/58/EC](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2002/58/oj) | Directive | Cookies, tracking, electronic marketing | Applied through each member state's law | 1 Nov 2026 |
| `EU-AIACT` | [AI Act (EU) 2024/1689](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj) | Regulation | Building, selling or using AI in the EU | Duties apply in phases. Check which apply on the review date, and any delay | 1 Nov 2026 |
| `EU-DSA` | [Digital Services Act (EU) 2022/2065](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2022/2065/oj) | Regulation | Platforms, marketplaces, hosting, online ads | Amends Directive 2000/31/EC | 1 Nov 2026 |
| `EU-DATA` | [Data Act (EU) 2023/2854](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/2854/oj) | Regulation | Connected products, data sharing, cloud switching | Fair access to and use of data | 1 Nov 2026 |

| `EU-UCPD` | [Unfair Commercial Practices Directive 2005/29/EC](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2005/29/oj) | Directive | Consumer marketing and sales | Amended since. Read the consolidated text | 1 Nov 2026 |
